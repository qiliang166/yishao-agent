PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026-08-02

一勺笔录(SOP)智能体 (YishaoAgent) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data
the application handles and how.

1. DATA STORED LOCALLY
   All project content, user accounts, settings, prompts, templates, generated documents,
   audio files, and images are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database and local
   file directories on your computer or server. No data is automatically uploaded to any
   cloud service.

2. WHAT WE COLLECT
   - User-provided content: project text, uploaded files, generated output, prompts
   - Account information: username, hashed password, permissions
   - Configuration: workspace settings, branding assets (logos)
   - Usage metadata: timestamps, download counts

3. WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT
   - No telemetry or analytics data is sent off-device
   - No usage tracking or behavioral profiling
   - No keystroke logging or screen capture
   - No automatic error reporting (unless explicitly enabled)

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
   The application may make API calls to third-party LLM (Large Language Model), TTS
   (Text-to-Speech), and image generation services that you configure. When you use
   these features, your prompt content is transmitted to those services. Please review
   the privacy policies of your chosen providers:
   - OpenAI (ChatGPT): https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
   - Other providers as configured by your administrator

5. DATA SECURITY
   - Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing (one-way)
   - JWT tokens are used for session management
   - Local data is stored in SQLite with WAL journal mode
   - We recommend enabling HTTPS for network deployments

6. YOUR RIGHTS
   - You own all content you create with the software
   - You may export and delete your data at any time
   - You may request assistance with data portability

7. CONTACT
   For privacy-related inquiries, please contact your software provider or administrator.

This application is designed for on-premises deployment. If your administrator has
configured additional data collection or cloud services, please refer to their
supplemental privacy notice.
